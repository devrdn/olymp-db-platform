# Images: sources and licences

Every image in the product is freely licensed. There is one rule and it has no
exceptions: **the file lives with us, and the attribution lives next to it.**
Hot-linking to somebody else's host is forbidden — it leaks participants'
addresses to a third party, and it is a page that breaks on the day that host
moves.

## Order of preference for licences

1. **CC0** — a waiver of rights effective worldwide. The preferred option.
2. **Public domain** — accepted, with a caveat: a work that entered the public
   domain under one country's law (a work of the United States government, say)
   has no guaranteed status in other jurisdictions. Wikimedia prints that caveat
   directly under such files.
3. **CC BY** — accepted only with the attribution line filled in.

Any licence carrying an `NC` or `ND` condition is not accepted: the platform is
a university one, but its terms of use are not "non-commercial" to the letter,
and processing a picture through the system (cropping, desaturating) is a
derivative work.

## Files

### `assets/archive-card-index.jpg`

- **Title:** Photograph of Storage Boxes, Used in National Archives Stack Area
- **Author:** General Services Administration. National Archives and Records
  Service. Office of Educational Programs. Education Division
- **Licence:** public domain (a work of the United States government)
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons, transferred by National Archives at College Park
  — Still Pictures as part of a cooperation project
- **Caveat:** may not be in the public domain outside the United States
- **Processing:** the 6445×3624 original is a scan of an archival mount — card
  stock on the left with the handwritten note "NA-406, Storage Boxes, Used in NA
  — Stock Areas", the pasted print on the right. Only the **print** is used in
  the product: cut from the 1920×1080 thumbnail at 1132×952 with offset
  (763, 10), then brought to 16:9 (1132×637) and saved as JPEG quality 80 with no
  metadata.
- **Subject:** an archive worker going through cards in a desktop card-index
  drawer labelled "Personal file, S to W"

Used as the demonstration picture on the case page in `preview.html`. The subject
was not chosen at random: a desktop card index is the direct ancestor of a row in
a relational table, and the whole direction is built on that kinship.

For real contests, CC0 material is preferred: the status of works of the United
States government is not guaranteed outside the United States (see the caveat
above).
